# Timeline

Budget: 10–12 hours/week ≈ 230–260 hours until launch. Development gets the largest
share, but planning, requirements and design together get five weeks on purpose — the
point of this project is to practise them, not rush past them.

Dates are a first draft. Revisit them at the end of every phase and record any change
(and why) in the change log at the bottom. Slipping is fine; slipping silently is not.

| Weeks | Dates (2026–27) | Phase | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 26 – Nov 1 | 1. Planning | Charter reviewed; 3–5 interviews done |
| 2–3 | Nov 2 – Nov 15 | 2. Requirements | MVP user stories with acceptance criteria, as Issues |
| 4–5 | Nov 16 – Nov 29 | 3. Design | Schema, API spec, wireframes, first ADRs |
| 6 | Nov 30 – Dec 6 | 4. Project setup | Repo scaffolded, board ready, sprint 1 planned |
| 7–14 | Dec 7 – Jan 31 | 5. Development (4 × 2-week sprints) | MVP features merged |
| 15–16 | Feb 1 – Feb 14 | 6. Testing + friends beta | No open P0/P1 bugs |
| 17–18 | Feb 15 – Feb 28 | 7–8. CI/CD + deployment | Production live, `v1.0.0` tagged |
| 19–20 | Mar 1 – Mar 14 | Buffer + launch prep | Leagues created for IPL; invites sent |
| — | IPL season | 9. Maintenance | Metrics tracked weekly; incidents written up |
| — | After IPL final | 10. Retrospective | Retro written; v1.1 planned |

Note: 2 weeks of the development window overlap with the Dec–Jan holidays. That is
a known risk — record it in the charter's risk register.
