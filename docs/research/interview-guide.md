# User Interview Guide

**Goal:** understand how cricket fans predict and compete with friends today, and what
would make them stick with an app for a whole season.

**Who:** 3–5 people who follow cricket and are in at least one group chat about it.
Try to include at least one person who would *organise* a league and one who would
only *play*.

**Length:** 10–15 minutes. Record notes in a copy of
[interview-template.md](interviews/interview-template.md), one file per person.

## Rules for yourself

- Ask about **past behaviour**, not hypothetical futures. "Last IPL, what did you do?"
  beats "Would you use an app that…?" — people are bad at predicting what they'd use.
- **Don't pitch the idea** until the last question. Once they know what you're
  building, they'll start being polite instead of honest.
- Ask "why?" and "tell me more about that" more than you think you should.
- Silence is fine. Let them fill it.
- Write down their **exact words** for anything surprising — quotes are evidence.

## Questions

### Warm-up (2 min)
1. Which cricket do you follow? IPL, internationals, both?
2. How do you usually watch — alone, with friends, at home, on your phone?

### Current behaviour (5 min)
3. Think back to the last IPL. Did you and your friends ever predict results or
   compete on who'd win? Walk me through how that worked.
4. Where did that happen — WhatsApp, a poll, a spreadsheet, in person?
5. Who kept track of who was right? How long did it last?
6. Have you used Dream11, MPL, or any fantasy or prediction app? What did you like
   or dislike? Do you still use it? Why / why not?

### Pain points (3 min)
7. What was annoying or fell apart about how your group did it?
8. If you stopped partway through the season, why?

### Motivation (3 min)
9. What made it fun when it was fun?
10. What would bragging rights look like in your group?

### Reaction (2 min) — only now, describe the idea in one sentence
11. "A free app where your group creates a private league, predicts each match, and
    sees a season leaderboard." — What's your first reaction?
12. What's the one thing it would *have* to do for you to use it all season?
13. Who in your group would set it up?

## After each interview

Within an hour, fill in the summary section of the notes: top 3 insights, the most
surprising thing, and any quotes. After all interviews, look across the notes for
patterns and bring them into section 1 (problem) and section 4 (scope) of the charter.
